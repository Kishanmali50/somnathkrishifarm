A founder's path that traded the classroom for the orchard — and never looked back.

co-Founder — Kishan Mali

My name is Kishan Mali, and my path into farming was anything but conventional.

I began a B.Tech in Food Technology at Sardarkrushinagar Dantiwada Agricultural University — but somewhere between the lecture halls and the fields around campus, I realised the classroom could teach me the theory of food and soil, while the orchard could teach me the practice. So I stepped away from the degree and stepped into the land instead, carrying that scientific curiosity about crops and quality straight into the orchard.

The road since has brought its own lessons — every season teaching patience, precision and a deeper respect for the fruit itself.

"Success is not about how fast you grow. It's about how much you learn while growing."

Today, that purpose goes beyond cultivation — it's about building a farm rooted in quality, knowledge and a mindset that keeps growing.
